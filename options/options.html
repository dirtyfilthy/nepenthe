What disappears

Not covered: search and form history (what you type into search boxes and forms). Firefox gives extensions no way to delete what's older than a limit (the Bad night button below can delete the last few hours), so turn it off in Firefox Settings → Privacy & Security → History → Customize history → Choose what you want Firefox to remember, then untick Remember search and form history. Leave browsing history on, because Nepenthe needs it.

Turning it off doesn't erase what's already saved. This button does, all of it at once. Saved passwords, addresses and cards aren't touched.

    Bad night

    Forget the last few hours right now. All history, downloads, recently closed tabs, and search and form history from that time are deleted. Sites you first visited in that time also lose their cookies and site data. Sites you'd visited before then keep theirs, so you stay signed in. This doesn't depend on the settings above, except that sites on the keep list keep their cookies.
