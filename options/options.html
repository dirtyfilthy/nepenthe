    Bad night

    Forget the last few hours right now.

    • All history, downloads, recently closed tabs, and search and form history from that time are deleted.
    • Sites you first visited in that time also lose their cookies and site data.
    • Sites you'd visited before then keep theirs, so you stay signed in.

    Search and form history

    What you type into search boxes and forms isn't covered. Firefox gives extensions no way to delete what's older than a limit (the Bad night button above can delete the last few hours), so turn it off in Firefox instead. Leave browsing history on, because Nepenthe needs it.

    How to stop Firefox saving it
    1. Open Firefox Settings and go to Privacy & Security.
    2. Under History, choose Customize history.
    3. Click Choose what you want Firefox to remember.
    4. Untick Remember search and form history. Leave Remember browsing and download history ticked.

    Turning it off doesn't erase what's already saved. This button does, all of it at once. Saved passwords, addresses and cards aren't touched.
